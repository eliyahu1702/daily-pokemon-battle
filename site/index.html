A Pokémon Emerald romhack

Daily Pokémon Battle

Draft six Pokémon with real competitive sets, then take on today's opponent. Everyone gets the same draft and the same battle, and there's a new one every day.

Play in your browser

You need your own copy of Pokémon Emerald (USA/Europe) as a .gba file. It's patched right here in your browser and never uploaded anywhere. Your save stays in this browser.

Controls

On a phone, use the on-screen buttons. On a keyboard: arrows to move, Z is A, X is B, Enter is Start, V is Select, Q and E are L and R. Use the gear icon under the game to change them, or plug in a controller.

Play on an emulator

  1. Download daily-pokemon-battle.bps.
  2. Patch your own Pokémon Emerald (USA/Europe) file with it. The easiest way is Rom Patcher JS, which works on phones too. On PC you can also use Flips.
  3. Open the patched file in an emulator with real-time clock support: mGBA on Windows, Mac or Linux, Pizza Boy GBA or My Boy! on Android, Delta on iPhone.

Base file check: CRC32 1F1C08FB, SHA-1 f3ae088181bf583e55daf962a92bb46f4f1d07b7. The new day starts at midnight on your device's clock.
